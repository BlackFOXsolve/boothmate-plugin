# BoothMate Guide

A beat-making coach for FL Studio, made for beginners and rappers. Ask Claude how to make a trap, drill, boom bap, R&B, lo-fi or Jersey club beat, and it walks you through it one step at a time: the tempo, the exact drum pattern for the step sequencer, four chords that fit your mood in any key, an 808 that follows the kick, a melody that fits, and how to record and treat your vocals.

## What it includes

- **beat-coach skill:** step-by-step coaching for FL Studio, with genre recipes (tempo and drum grids) and mood chord progressions in its reference files.

## Want Claude to build the beat for you?

The full **BoothMate** plugin connects Claude directly to FL Studio and does the clicking for you. It's free at https://github.com/BlackFOXsolve/boothmate-plugin.

## What this plugin runs

Nothing. BoothMate Guide is instructions and reference text only. It runs no programs, installs nothing, connects to no servers, and reads no files on your computer.

## Privacy Policy

BoothMate Guide collects no personal data. It has no accounts, analytics or telemetry, stores nothing, and sends nothing anywhere. Your conversations with Claude are handled by Anthropic under Anthropic's own privacy policy. Questions: contact@blackfoxsolve.com, BlackAcre AI LLC.
