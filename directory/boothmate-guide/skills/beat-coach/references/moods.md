# Mood progressions

Each mood is four chords, one bar each. Degrees are scale steps (1 = the key's root). Examples are in C.

## Dark

- Scale: harmonic minor
- Degrees: 1 - 6 - 4 - 5
- i - VI - iv - V. Classic dark trap tension.
- Example in C minor: Cm | G# | Fm | G

## Sad

- Scale: minor
- Degrees: 1 - 6 - 3 - 7
- i - VI - III - VII. Emotional, melodic rap.
- Example in C minor: Cm | G# | D# | A#

## Menacing

- Scale: phrygian
- Degrees: 1 - 2 - 1 - 6
- i - bII - i - VI. Drill-style menace.
- Example in C minor: Cm | C# | Cm | G#

## Hype

- Scale: minor
- Degrees: 1 - 7 - 6 - 7
- i - VII - VI - VII. Driving, energetic.
- Example in C minor: Cm | A# | G# | A#

## Chill

- Scale: dorian
- Degrees: 1 - 4 - 1 - 5
- i7 - IV7 - i7 - v7. Smooth lo-fi / boom bap.
- Example in C minor: Cm7 | F7 | Cm7 | Gm7

## Love

- Scale: major
- Degrees: 1 - 6 - 4 - 5
- Imaj7 - vi7 - IVmaj7 - V7. Warm R&B.
- Example in C major: Cmaj7 | Am7 | Fmaj7 | G7

## Happy

- Scale: major
- Degrees: 1 - 5 - 6 - 4
- I - V - vi - IV. Bright pop-rap.
- Example in C major: C | G | Am | F

## 808 rule

Put one 808 note on every kick hit, pitched to the root of the chord playing at that moment. Hold it until the next kick (at most one bar). Keep it between A1 and G#2 in standard MIDI naming (A2 to G#3 in FL Studio's labels).
