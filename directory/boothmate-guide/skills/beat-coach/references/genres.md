# Genre recipes

Step grids are 16 steps per bar (one step = a 1/16 note). `x` = hit, `.` = rest. Two-bar patterns show bar 1 then bar 2. The fill replaces the last bar of a 4-bar loop.

## Trap

Half-time snare on beat 3, rolling hats, 808 follows the kick.

- Tempo: 140 BPM
- Default mood: dark
- 808 slides: no

| Sound | Bar 1 | Bar 2 |
|---|---|---|
| kick | `x.........x.....` | `x.....x...x.....` |
| snare | `........x.......` | `........x.......` |
| clap | `........x.......` | `........x.......` |
| hat | `x.x.x.x.x.x.x.x.` | `x.x.x.x.xxx.x.x.` |
| open hat | `..............x.` | `..............x.` |

Fill (last bar): hat `xxxxx.x.xxx.xxxx`, snare `........x.....xx`
