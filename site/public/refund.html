Beat Breakdown refund policy

Last updated November 24, 2026. Beat Breakdown is sold by BlackAcre AI LLC. Payments are handled by Stripe.

Automatic refunds

If we can't complete your breakdown (for example, the video can't be opened or our analyser fails three times), we refund your full payment automatically. You don't need to do anything; the results page tells you when it's done. Refunds usually show on your card in 5 to 10 business days.

Delivered breakdowns

A breakdown is a digital product delivered as soon as it appears on your results page. Delivered breakdowns are final and aren't refunded for a change of mind, because the analysis has already been run and paid for on our side. We do review requests when something actually went wrong:

  • You were charged more than once for the same breakdown.
  • The results are for a different video than the one you paid for.
  • The results page is empty, broken, or clearly not about music.
  • You paid but never received a results page.

Results are estimates of what the analyser hears; a tempo or key that's slightly different from what you expected isn't a fault on its own. Send a request within 14 days of purchase. We review every request against this policy and reply by email.
